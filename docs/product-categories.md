# Sulan Peptide Product Categories

Official catalog: [https://sulanpeptide.net/products/](https://sulanpeptide.net/products/)

Sulan Peptide organizes research-use peptide products by research direction to make product discovery and documentation review easier.

## Categories

### Metabolic Research

Products commonly reviewed in metabolic, weight-management, appetite-regulation, incretin, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Repair & Recovery Research

Products commonly reviewed in tissue-repair, recovery, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Growth Research

Products commonly reviewed in growth hormone axis, GH secretagogue, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Skin & Cosmetic Research

Products commonly reviewed in skin, cosmetic, copper peptide, collagen, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Mitochondrial Research

Products commonly reviewed in mitochondrial function, cellular energy, oxidative stress, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Neuropeptide Research

Products commonly reviewed in neuropeptide, melanocortin, cognitive, and related laboratory research contexts.

### Aging Research

Products commonly reviewed in aging biology, cellular senescence, telomere, and related laboratory research contexts.
