# Sulan Peptide

**Sulan Peptide** supplies research-use peptide products with categorized specifications, quotation support, and selected batch documentation records.

Official website: [https://sulanpeptide.net/](https://sulanpeptide.net/)

COA & Batch Records: [https://sulanpeptide.net/coa/](https://sulanpeptide.net/coa/)

## About

Sulan Peptide focuses on research-use peptide product organization, clear product specifications, buyer-side documentation support, and selected Janoshik-tested batch records where available.

The public website is designed to help buyers review:

- Product categories
- Product specifications
- Available COA and batch record information
- Janoshik task number and unique key details where reports are available
- Research-use product documentation context

## Product Categories

Sulan Peptide organizes its catalog by research direction:

- Metabolic Research
- Repair & Recovery Research
- Growth Research
- Skin & Cosmetic Research
- Mitochondrial Research
- Neuropeptide Research
- Aging Research

See: [docs/product-categories.md](docs/product-categories.md)

## COA & Batch Documentation

Selected Sulan Peptide products have publicly displayed COA and batch record snapshots. Available records may include product name, purity, batch reference, Janoshik task number, and unique key details.

See: [docs/coa-batch-records.md](docs/coa-batch-records.md)
